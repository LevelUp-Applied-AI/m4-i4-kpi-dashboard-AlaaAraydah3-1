## KPI 1
- **Name:** Total Revenue
- **Definition:** Total revenue generated by all orders in the Amman Digital Market.
- **Formula:** SUM(quantity * unit_price) over all valid orders
- **Data Source (tables/columns):** order_items.quantity, products.unit_price, orders.order_id
    Baseline Value: Sum of all revenue in the merged dataset (computed in code)
- **Interpretation:** Higher values indicate stronger overall sales performance.

## KPI 2
- **Name:** Average Order Value (AOV)
- **Definition:** Average revenue per order across the platform.
- **Formula:** SUM(revenue per order) / Number of orders
- **Data Source (tables/columns):** order_items.quantity, products.unit_price, orders.order_id
- **Baseline Value**: Mean of grouped revenue by order_id
- **Interpretation:** Higher AOV indicates customers are spending more per order.

## KPI 3
- **Name:** Customer Retention Rate
- **Definition:** Percentage of customers who made more than one purchase.
- **Formula:** (Number of customers with >1 order / Total customers) * 100
- **Data Source (tables/columns):** orders.customer_id, orders.order_id
- **Baseline Value:** Computed retention rate from dataset
- **Interpretation:** Higher percentage shows better repeat purchase behavior.

## KPI 4
- **Name:** Monthly Active Users
- **Definition:** Number of unique customers placing orders per month.
- **Formula:** COUNT(DISTINCT customer_id) per month
- **Data Source (tables/columns):** orders.customer_id, orders.order_date
- **Baseline Value:** Monthly count of active users from merged dataset
- **Interpretation:** Increasing trend shows growing engagement; decreasing trend may indicate churn.

## KPI 5
- **Name:** Cohort Revenue Growth
- **Definition:** Total revenue per customer cohort defined by registration month.
- **Formula:** SUM(revenue) grouped by registration_month
- **Data Source (tables/columns):** orders.order_date, customers.registration_date, order_items.quantity, products.unit_price
- **Baseline Value:** Cohort revenue sums as computed in merged dataset
- **Interpretation:** Helps track revenue growth from newly acquired customers; higher values indicate stronger cohort performance.
