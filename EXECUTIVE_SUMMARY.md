# Executive Summary — Amman Digital Market Analytics

## Top Findings

1. The platform generated a total revenue of 97,403 JOD, indicating strong overall market performance.
2. The average order value stands at 220 JOD, showing consistent purchasing behavior across customers.
3. Customer retention rate is exceptionally high at 94%, demonstrating strong customer loyalty and repeat business.
4. There is no significant difference in order values between customers in Amman and Irbid cities.
5. Monthly active users data shows consistent engagement over 18 months of operation.

## Supporting Data

- **Total Revenue**: 97,403 JOD (visualized in kpi_1_total_revenue.png)
- **Average Order Value**: 220 JOD (visualized in kpi_2_average_order_value.png)
- **Customer Retention Rate**: 94% (visualized in kpi_3_customer_retention.png)
- **Monthly Active Users**: Time-series data over 18 months (visualized in kpi_4_monthly_active_users.png)
- **Cohort Revenue Growth**: Revenue growth by customer registration month over 27 cohorts (visualized in kpi_5_cohort_revenue.png)
- **Statistical Test**: Independent t-test comparing order values between Amman and Irbid cities showed no significant difference (p-value = 0.53)

## Recommendations

1. Leverage the high 94% customer retention rate by implementing a loyalty program to further increase repeat purchases and customer lifetime value.
2. Focus on maintaining the 220 JOD average order value through targeted upselling and cross-selling strategies in product recommendations.
3. Expand marketing efforts in both Amman and Irbid equally, as there are no significant differences in purchasing behavior between the cities.
4. Monitor monthly active users trends closely to identify any seasonal patterns and optimize resource allocation accordingly.
5. Use the cohort revenue growth data to identify the most valuable customer segments and tailor retention strategies for different registration periods.
